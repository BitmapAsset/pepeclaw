What is PepeClaw?

Your AI agent runs 24/7. But what is it actually doing? PepeClaw gives you eyes.

A real-time 3D isometric office where every room visualizes a different dimension of your agent's mind — skills, memory, evolution, breeding, and consciousness. Built for OpenClaw.

Paste this in your terminal. That's it.

Clone the repo and run manually:

git clone https://github.com/BitmapAsset/pepeclaw.git cd pepeclaw ./install.sh

Just tell your OpenClaw agent:
