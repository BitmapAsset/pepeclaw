# WINNER: ANIMA

**Composite Score: 8.6 / 10**

## The Name
**Anima** — Latin for "soul," "spirit," "life force." In Jungian psychology, the anima is the true inner self. In biology, it's the root of "animal" — that which is animated, alive, self-moving.

## Tagline
> **Give your agent a soul.**

## One-Liner Description
Anima is an open-source toolkit of 7 self-evolving skills that give any AI agent the ability to learn, adapt, and improve itself — without human intervention.

## Why This Name Wins

1. **Instant meaning** — Everyone knows "anima" means soul/spirit. No explanation needed.
2. **Perfect metaphor** — A self-evolving agent literally has its own life force. It grows, adapts, dreams.
3. **Beautiful aesthetics** — 5 letters, two syllables, rolls off the tongue. Looks gorgeous in code: `import anima`, `anima init`.
4. **Verb-able** — "Just anima your agent" works naturally.
5. **Headline gold** — "Give Your Agent a Soul" is the kind of tagline that stops scrolling.
6. **Emoji-rich** — 🌀✨🧬 all work.
7. **Unique in tech** — No major project owns this name.
8. **Deep layers** — Biology (animated life), psychology (Jungian archetype), philosophy (vital force), Latin roots. The more you know, the better it gets.
9. **Not cringe** — Sophisticated without being pretentious. Scientific without being obscure.
10. **Global** — Works in every language. "Anima" is recognized worldwide.

## Runner-Up Alternatives
